## About Sprintlet

Sprintlet is an enterprise issue-tracking platform designed to help software teams plan, track, and ship better software.

- **Affordable:** Enterprise-grade issue tracking without enterprise-grade pricing.
- **Focused:** Built around software development and issue tracking, not hundreds of unrelated features.
- **Fast:** A responsive experience that stays out of your team's way.
- **AI slop free:** No pointless AI features added just because they're fashionable. Automation and intelligence should solve real problems.
- **Enterprise ready:** Built with the reliability, security, and scalability that professional teams expect.
- **Developer focused:** Designed around the workflows engineering teams actually use.

## What You Can Do

- Sprintlet gives teams a central place to manage their software development workflow:
- Create and organize issues.
- Plan and manage sprints.
- Track projects and milestones.
- Assign work across teams.
- Prioritize and organize backlogs.
- Monitor progress and workflow.
- Collaborate around issues.
- Manage software development at scale.
- Whether you're running a small engineering team or coordinating development across an enterprise, Sprintlet keeps your work organized without getting in the way.
